## Setup

1. Were you able to install the app from the Play Store test link?
2. Did the app open without crashing?
3. Did light and dark mode look readable?

## Student Flow

1. Could you create or sign in to a student account?
2. Could you enter exam dates, study pace, subjects, and topics?
3. Did the generated timetable make sense?
4. Could you mark a study session as done?
5. Did the recall proof step feel clear?

## Parent Flow

1. Could you create or sign in to a parent account?
2. Could you link a student?
3. Could you view student progress?
4. Did parent messages feel helpful and not overwhelming?

## Reminders And Missed Sessions

1. Did reminders ask for permission clearly?
2. Did the test notification appear on your phone?
3. Did the app show reminders as allowed and scheduled?
4. Did missed-session or catch-up messages make sense?
