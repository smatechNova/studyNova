## Asset Policy

- Use custom StudyNova-owned artwork for the logo, app icon, adaptive icon, splash screen, and notification icon.
- Use stock photography only for hero panels, sign-in cards, onboarding panels, Play Store screenshots, and presentation materials.
- Prefer images with no visible school logos, no sensitive student details, and no unnecessary identifiable children.
- Do not use stock photos as proof of real student activity.
- For paid assets, store license receipts outside the public repo and record the source here.
